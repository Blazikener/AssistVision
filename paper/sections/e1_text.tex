\textbf{Relative models: anchoring on the table fails from overhead.} The five
relative models have a median volume error of 88.4--99.4\,\% under the
deployable anchor protocol (Table~\ref{tab:n5k}), even though their predicted
table is flat to 0.6--0.7\,mm RMS: the scale-and-shift fit reproduces the table
and collapses the food relief. The fit is badly conditioned on most dishes
(Fig.~\ref{fig:cond}), as expected when the only reference is a plane almost
perpendicular to the optical axis. This is the quantitative reason the rig
views the plate at $45^\circ$ and uses the marker mat and multiple views.

\textbf{Metric models are not metric at plate distance, but one scale helps.}
Used as-is, all five metric models are far off: AbsRel 1.33--2.76 and median
volume errors of 759--801\,\% (DA-V2 metric indoor) and 3{,}539--5{,}328\,\%
(ZoeDepth). ZoeDepth places the table at about 1.2\,m instead of 0.35\,m, a
distance well outside the room-scale scenes it was trained on. Their anchor
fits only one scale to the table, which is well conditioned even from
overhead. ZoeDepth-NYU is then the best deployable model: 44.1\,\% [39.6,
50.5] median volume error, 24\,\% of dishes within 20\,\%, ahead of the next
model (DA-V2-Metric-Indoor-S, 73.5\,\%; Holm-corrected Wilcoxon
$p=3.6\times10^{-9}$). That is still an order of magnitude worse than
multi-view reconstruction (Sec.~\ref{sec:synth}). Its predicted table is not
flat (9.4\,mm RMS), and the KITTI-mixed variant is worse (58.6\,\%). Model size
does not order the DA-V2 metric models: B is the worst of the three under
every protocol.

\textbf{Depth metrics do not predict volume.} After alignment AbsRel is
0.013--0.125 for every model, yet volume errors span 10--100\,\%. Food relief
(1--3\,cm) is a small fraction of the 36\,cm camera distance, so a model can
score well on depth and still lose most of the volume. We therefore rank
models on volume, not on AbsRel.

\textbf{Oracle upper bound.} With an oracle fit DA-V2-L is best of all ten
models: 25.9\,\% [23.7, 30.2] median volume error with its own support plane
and 9.6\,\% with the sensor's plane. Within Depth Anything V2 (relative) the
error falls with encoder size, from 37.4\,\% (S) to 32.1\,\% (B) to 25.9\,\%
(L); L is separated from B with Holm-corrected $p=3.1\times10^{-19}$. The gap
between the two plane choices shows that fitting the support plane in
predicted depth causes more than half of the error; in the rig the marker mat
provides this plane independently of the network. The oracle needs food
ground truth and is not deployable.

\textbf{Coverage and failures.} 498 of the 503 usable dishes were evaluated
for every model. The remaining five have no visible table (degenerate plane
fit) and are counted as failures, not dropped. Latency is 1.4--3.9\,s per
image on 8-vCPU cloud VMs (VM evidence): DA-V1-S, DA-V2-S/B and MiDaS ran on a
machine shared with other jobs, so their times are upper bounds, and the other
models ran alone on identical VMs. Depth Pro is implemented in the same
benchmark but had not finished at the time of writing.
