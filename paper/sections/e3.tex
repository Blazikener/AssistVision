\textbf{Scenes.} Twenty-two Blender (Cycles) scenes with watertight food meshes
whose volume is computed exactly from the mesh: eight food configurations
(single rice mound, rice + dal, two idli, curry heap, lumpy rice, a three-item
thali, a 40\,mL small portion and a tall mound) on ceramic and polished steel
plates, four textureless variants and two on banana leaf. Each scene is rendered
at $1600\times1200$, $60^\circ$ horizontal FoV, $45^\circ$ elevation and
\SI{0.40}{m} distance, with the plate rotating on a turntable inside a printed
50\,mm ArUco mat, i.e.\ the rig geometry. View 0 also stores a metric depth map,
which gives the ``perfect single-view depth'' row: the error a monocular method
would have even if its depth were exact, because it cannot see behind the food.

\textbf{Methods.} (i) Dense MVS: COLMAP SfM, marker-triangulated scale,
OpenMVS densification, plate-noise-calibrated footprint threshold, shared
integral. (ii) Sparse SfM points only, same integral. (iii) Monocular depth on
view 0 under the anchor and oracle protocols, with the plane taken from the
marker-mat pose.
