Across 12 served/cleared pairs, the eaten share from the glasses sweep is off by a median of 10.7 percentage points with the fixed footprint rule used as the main rule in E5, and 5/12 pairs fall within 10 points (Table~\ref{tab:e6}). The same plates on the turntable path give 2.8 points. The plate matters: on steel the median miss is 8.0 points (6 pairs), on white ceramic 16.5 points (6 pairs). The served scan alone is off by a median of +12.1\,\% in volume on steel and -25.2\,\% on ceramic, because a 60-degree sweep from one seat never sees the far side of the food, and, as in E3, white ceramic gives fewer features to match. Where the served and cleared scans are biased by different amounts, the bias does not cancel in the ratio. The largest miss is idli pair on ceramic (-106.1 points). The region-growing footprint, the second rule from E5, gives 3.4 points (8/12 within 10); we report it but did not choose it in advance. These are rendered scenes with exact volumes and a simulated head path, not measurements from the printed glasses.
