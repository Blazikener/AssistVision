Even sensor-depth volume with a cross-validated density predicts mass worse
than ignoring the image and predicting the mean (Table~\ref{tab:mass}).
Sensor volume and weighed mass are only weakly correlated (Pearson $r=0.19$,
Spearman $\rho=0.17$, $n=503$). Mixed dishes differ in density, and the
dish region includes the plate rim. A single image-derived volume therefore
cannot stand in for grams on real dishes, and this is the quantitative case for
weighing the plate in the rig. Mass counts $n$ vary because dishes with a
non-positive predicted volume are excluded from the density fit.
