\emph{These are partial-run results: seven of eleven planned depth models and
11 of 22 synthetic scenes had finished at the time of writing.} Deployable
table-only anchoring of relative models fails from an overhead view (median
volume error $\geq$92.7\,\%), because a fronto-parallel plane cannot fix both
scale and shift, and metric indoor models used as-is overestimate volume
roughly eightfold at plate distance. With an oracle fit the best model (DA-V2-B) reproduces sensor
volume to 13.0\,\% median error when the sensor's support plane is used, but
to only 32.1\,\% when the plane is fitted in its own prediction.
Dense 24-view MVS with marker scale reaches 3.9\,\% [1.0, 5.7] median absolute
error against exact mesh volume, while sparse SfM alone misses 92.4\,\%. Accuracy collapses below 12 views and under
heavy JPEG compression, but not at half resolution. Even sensor-depth volume predicts weighed mass
worse than the dataset mean (68.2 vs 52.0\,\% median error), which is why the
rig weighs the plate. No result in this paper comes from the physical rig.
